What gets in

A story is listed if the model rates it at least 6 out of 10 for being about climate change — its causes, its effects or the response to it. A weather story makes it in when it connects to climate records or trends; a plain forecast doesn't. Stories are never copied: the site shows the headline, a one-sentence summary written by the model, and the outlet.

Paywalls

Outlets marked $ usually require a subscription. The site doesn't try to get around that. Where another outlet covered the same story, it appears under "Also", and free sources are listed first.
